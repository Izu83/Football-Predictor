What the numbers mean

Chance is the probability of each result. On 2024/25 we tested every mix of our model and the bookmaker's price (margin removed). The bookmaker on its own was the most accurate, so when bet365 odds are available the chance shown is their price with the margin removed. The model's own numbers are under Details. Without odds, the model's chance is shown. Expected goals, likely scores and over/under always come from the model.

Pick is the most likely of eight simple outcomes: home win, away win, home or draw, away or draw, over or under 2.5 goals, and whether both teams score. Outcomes above 85% are skipped because they pay almost nothing. High, Medium and Low describe how likely the pick is. Each card shows how often picks at that level won on a season the model had never seen.

Winning often is not the same as profit. A pick that wins 78% of the time at odds of 1.25 still loses money in the long run. The bookmaker's margin is built into every price.

Model edges are bets where the model disagrees with the bookmaker enough to expect a profit. On the test season those bets lost about 9%, and even the most optimistic end of the range was a loss. So they are switched off and will come back only if a future backtest shows they are profitable.

How the model works

  • About four seasons of results from 61 competitions: the Champions League, Europa League and Conference League with their qualifiers, plus top divisions, second divisions and smaller European leagues. All data comes from FotMob.
  • A Dixon-Coles goals model gives each team an attack and a defence rating, with recent matches counting more. Each team's rating is pulled toward its league's average, and European games put every league on one scale.
  • Each competition has its own home advantage. Every possible scoreline gets a probability, and all the markets are worked out from those.

How it was tested

Each week the model was refitted using only matches played before that week, then it predicted that week's matches. Every setting was chosen on 2024/25 and then measured once on 2025/26, which played no part in choosing it. The Track record tab shows those results, plus every prediction published on this page since tracking began, scored on the real results.

Please bet responsibly

No model can tell you what will happen in a football match. The bookmaker's margin means the average bet loses money. Only stake what you can afford to lose. If gambling stops being fun, get help from a support service in your country. 18+.
