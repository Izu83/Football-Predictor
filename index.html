How good is it?

Walk-forward backtest over 2025-08-01 to 2026-06-01: the model was refitted every week using only matches played before that week.

Picking the most likely result was right 51% of the time across 7,482 matches. On the big five leagues the model's log loss was 0.998 against 0.978 for bet365's own prices (lower is better). The bookmaker was more accurate than the model.

Value bets with the current settings (35% model weight, 5% minimum edge): 308 bets, 35% won, ROI -6.3% at bet365 pre-match odds.

How it works: a Dixon-Coles goals model rates every team's attack and defence from about four seasons of results in 28 competitions (FotMob data), weighting recent matches more. Team ratings are pulled towards their league's average, and European matches put all the leagues on one scale. "Value" means the blend of model (35%) and bookmaker (65%) gives at least 5% expected profit. "Lean" is the most likely simple outcome.
